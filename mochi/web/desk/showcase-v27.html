Methods and recording provenance

All task-relevant motion comes from MuJoCo contact simulation. The second and third videos render recorded body transforms with the current viewer. Neither moves the physical robot through animation. Every task has a numerical success check.

The 804-to-470 comparison isolates post-training under the V15 implementation. The final configuration includes later controller engineering and a different scenario, so 470-to-399 is not a matched training result. The final run reproduces a previously evaluated scenario; it is not a new generalization test or proof of a globally minimal step count.

Tinker supplies post-training and saved-model sampling. Exa supplies sources for measured failure investigations. past.dev carries observations and results between workflow roles. Modal runs simulation batches and training infrastructure. Rendering these three videos did not require new model training.
